Help & support

We usually reply within a day or two.

Contact us

Email WearAboutApp@gmail.com with questions, bugs, ideas, or anything about your account or data.

Signing in

Enter your email and we'll send you a 6-digit code. If it doesn't arrive in a minute or two, check your spam or promotions folder, then tap to send a new one.

Adding friends

Go to Friends and share your invite link, or type a friend's code. You see each other's posts once you've both added each other.

My photo wasn't scored or has no pieces

Try a full-length photo in good light where your whole outfit is visible. You can add or rename pieces yourself on the fit's page.

Notifications

Turn them on in Profile. On iPhone, notifications only work in the installed app (or from the home-screen icon), not in a Safari tab.

Report or block someone

Tap ••• on their post, then Report post or Block. We review every report within 24 hours. Blocking ends the friendship and stops them from adding you again. You can unblock in Profile → Privacy & data.

Turn off photo analysis

Profile → Privacy & data → Turn off. We'll ask again before your next fit.

Delete your account

Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your fits, photos, closet, posts and friends. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
