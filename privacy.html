Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version
  • Your fit photos, scores and closet are private to you. Friends only see what you choose to post, and only mutual friends can see it.
  • To score your outfit and make backdrops, your fit photos are sent to two AI services, Anthropic and Photoroom, only after you agree in the app, and with faces covered first.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Profile.

Who we are

WearAbout ("we", "us") is an app for logging your daily outfits, getting style feedback, and sharing fits with close friends. If you have questions about this policy or your data, email us at WearAboutApp@gmail.com.

AI processing of your photos

Before your first fit, the app asks for your permission to send fit photos to these services. If you don't agree, no photos are sent to them. You can turn this off any time in Profile → Privacy & data.

  • Anthropic (Claude) analyzes each fit photo and the details you add to score the outfit, suggest the occasion, list the pieces you're wearing, and suggest tweaks or pieces to buy.
  • Photoroom removes the background from each fit photo so we can place you on a studio or themed backdrop.

Faces are covered before photos leave your phone. Before a fit photo is sent to Anthropic or Photoroom, the app finds faces on your device and covers them with a mosaic. When a backdrop comes back, the app puts your real face back, also on your device. The covered-up copies are the only versions these services receive.

These companies process your photos on our behalf only to provide these features, under their own terms and privacy policies.

No facial recognition or biometric data

WearAbout does not use facial recognition, does not identify anyone from their face, and does not create, store, or share face templates, face geometry, or any other biometric identifiers. Face finding runs only on your device, only to cover faces before upload, and the face positions it finds are discarded as soon as the photo is processed. They're never saved or sent to us or anyone else.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of service providers to run WearAbout:

  • Supabase stores your account, data and photos, and runs our server code.
  • Anthropic and Photoroom, as described above.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's web files, jsDelivr delivers code libraries (including the on-device face finder), and Google Fonts provides the app's typeface. Like any website, they receive your IP address when the app loads.
  • Google (Gmail) sends your sign-in code emails.
  • Apple, Google and other browser push services deliver notifications you turn on.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or others. If we find content that sexualizes a child, we preserve it and report it, along with the account's details, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), as US law requires.

What friends can see

Your fits, scores, closet, style goal and recommendations are private. When you post a fit, your mutual friends (people you've both added) can see the post photo, caption, any piece you plug, any chat invitation, and hearts. Your score is never shown to anyone else. Your phone number is visible only to mutual friends, and only if you add it.

Shopping links

"Shop it" and buy suggestions open a search or store website. That website's own privacy policy applies once you leave WearAbout. If we join affiliate programs in the future, we may earn a commission on purchases, and we'll label those links.

How long we keep data

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. When you remove a post or fit, it's deleted from our database and storage. When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, fits, photos, closet, recommendations, posts, friendships and settings. Copies in service-provider backups may remain for a short period before being overwritten. Reports you filed may be kept so we can keep our community safe.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete any fit, post, closet item, name, photo or phone number in the app.
  • Turn off photo analysis in Profile → Privacy & data.
  • Block or report anyone from the ••• menu on a post.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete account.
  • Request a copy of your data, or ask us anything about it, by emailing WearAboutApp@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, deletion, and portability. We honor these requests for everyone. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Photos are stored in private storage and shown only through short-lived links. Database rules make sure people can only see their own data and their mutual friends' posts. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

WearAbout is for adults 18 and older. Everyone confirms their age when they sign up, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll let you know in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
